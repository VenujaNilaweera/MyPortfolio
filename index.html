01 About

I build embedded and automation systems end to end. Across robotics, thermal sensing, machine-vision inspection and wearable biomedical devices, my work follows the same path: design the mechanics in SolidWorks, lay out the electronics and PCB, write the real-time firmware, tune the control loops, and fabricate a prototype that actually runs. Two of my projects are currently going through the patent filing process, and my prototypes have been exhibited nationally at Techno Exhibition 2025 (BMICH) and IEEE Innovation Nation Sri Lanka 2026. ◈ Motion-Controlled Machine Vision PATENT FILING IN PROGRESS

Automated Parasitic Egg Detection System

Scanning Automation & Machine Vision Inspection · 2025
  • Automated microscope slide scanning with NEMA 17 steppers and A4988 drivers on an Arduino Nano — zig-zag traversal, grid-dimension calculation and home-position return.

04 Final-Year Research
◈ Wearable Biomedical System

Dengue Critical-Phase Early Warning

Undergraduate Research · Team Member · Dept. of Electrical & Electronic Engineering · 2026–Present
  • ▸Wearable system that continuously monitors confirmed dengue patients and flags the critical-phase transition earlier than periodic clinical blood tests — fusing PPG heart rate, oscillometric blood pressure, skin temperature and automated Capillary Refill Time (CRT).
  • ▸Novel automated CRT actuation module — a pneumatic pump-solenoid pressure system (four Arduino-driven IRFZ44N MOSFETs) driving a syringe-based fingertip actuator for repeatable, operator-independent nail-bed compression and release.
  • ▸Automated image-processing pipeline (LAB colour-space conversion, watershed segmentation, ellipse-based ROI refinement) tracks the a* channel colour-recovery curve to extract CRT; wrist-worn module built on a custom PCB and validated against a clinical perception study.
